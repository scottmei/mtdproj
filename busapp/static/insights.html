    Where and when are buses late?

    Lateness by line, stop, hour and day of week, and whose estimate is closest: the schedule, MTD's live time, or our model.

    Average delay is observed departure minus scheduled time; bars grow right for late and left for early from the zero line. The whisker is a 95% confidence interval clustered by bus trip: a late bus is late at every stop it serves, so its stops are not independent evidence.

    Schedule / MTD / Our error are average errors (mean absolute error) on the same departures within each group: those where MTD's estimate from the chosen horizon was captured and our model had earlier-day history. The closest estimate is in bold. "—" means no departures qualify yet, typically a service pattern with no earlier day to learn from.
